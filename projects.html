Research

Our collaboration connects generative diffusion models with quantum field theory, lattice simulations and stochastic dynamics. Explore the research directions below, with links to publications and open-source software.

Physics of Diffusion Models

  • Stochastic quantization: connecting diffusion models to Langevin dynamics and using them to sample scalar lattice fields. JHEP 05 (2024) 060 · DMasSQ notebook.
  • Beyond Gaussian statistics: understanding how diffusion models preserve and learn higher-order cumulants. Machine Learning: Science and Technology 6 (2025) 025004.

Diffusion Models for Lattice Simulations

  • Scalar fields near criticality: diffusion sampling of two- and three-dimensional φ4 theory, with diagnostics and generalization to unseen lattice volumes. arXiv:2607.08505 (2026) · DM-for-phi4 code.
  • Abelian gauge fields: physics-conditioned sampling of two-dimensional U(1) gauge theory, including topology and transfer across couplings and lattice sizes. JHEP 03 (2026) 111.
  • Non-Abelian gauge fields: gauge equivariant diffusion models for U(2) and SU(2) lattice gauge theories. arXiv:2601.19552 (2026) · DM_U2_LGT code for U(2).

Sampling Algorithms and Complex Actions

  • Metropolis-adjusted annealed Langevin sampling: combining learned scores with acceptance steps for lattice gauge simulations. U(1) study · Non-Abelian study.
  • Complex Langevin and diffusion models: learning distributions generated by complex Langevin dynamics with score-based and energy-based models. JHEP 12 (2025) 160.

Physics-Informed Kernels

  • Generative sampling based on renormalization group kernels, with independently learned propagation between network layers. arXiv:2510.26678 (2025).

Non-Equilibrium Stochastic Dynamics

  • Learning transition kernels from short-time configuration pairs to predict long-time dynamics, including critical relaxation, coarsening and driven colloids. arXiv:2610.00546 (2026).
