Research

Our collaboration explores several interconnected research directions that combine generative models with quantum field theories. A brief overview of ongoing projects is given below.

Physics of Diffusion Models

  • Stochastic quantization and Langevin simulations (2023) – building connection between stochastic quantization and diffusion models.
  • Statistics of diffusion models (2024) – characterizing the probabilistic structure of diffusion processes.
  • Self‑consistency checks and configurational temperature (in preparation).
  • Learning dynamics and the renormalization group (in preparation).
  • New stochastic quantization schemes (in preparation).

Diffusion Models for Lattice Simulations

  • 2D scalar field simulations (2023).
  • 2D gauge field simulations (2024–2025) – U(1) and U(N)/SU(N) gauge theories on the lattice.
  • 3D scalar field simulations (in preparation).
  • 4D gauge field simulations (in preparation).

High‑Efficiency Samplers

  • Metropolis‑adjusted annealed Langevin algorithms (2024).
  • Energy‑based diffusion models for sign‑problem mitigation (2025).
  • Physics‑conditioned samplers for critical slowing down (in preparation).

Other Topics

  • Renormalization group and diffusion model correspondence (proposal).
  • Physics-informed kernels methods (2025).
  • Energy‑based renormalization group diffusion models (in preparation).
  • Explorations of further connections between machine learning and field theory.
