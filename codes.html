Codes & Software

The DM‑QFT Collaboration develops and maintains software for lattice simulations, generative models and data analysis. Our public codebases include:

  • DMasSQ – a self-contained notebook demonstrating diffusion models as stochastic quantization for scalar φ4 lattice field theory.
  • DM-for-phi4 – diffusion-model training, sampling and analysis for two- and three-dimensional scalar φ4 lattice field theory.
